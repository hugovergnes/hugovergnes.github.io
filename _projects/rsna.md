Technical recall is when a screening mammogram has to be repeated because of an
imaging problem — positioning, compression, motion — rather than anything clinical.
It costs the patient a return visit and the clinic a slot, and it is assessed by
radiologists today.

This study compared AI against expert radiologists on that assessment. The work was
accepted for scientific presentation at **RSNA 2024**.

It came out of my time at Whiterabbit.ai on WRClear, a software-as-a-medical-device
combining autonomous and assistive AI for screening mammography, where I developed and
evaluated models across several clinical tasks and led the statistical study design
used to demonstrate the device's benefits to the FDA.
